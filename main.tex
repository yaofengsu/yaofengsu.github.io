%-----------RESEARCH INTERESTS-----------
\section{Research Interests}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.10in, label={}, topsep=0pt]
\item\small My undergraduate research has centered on building faithful representations of the world with multimodal generative models. Through diffusion distillation, reinforcement learning, and structured memory and agent harnesses, my work across streaming audio-visual synthesis, risk-aware scene modeling, and lifelong memory has laid the groundwork for real-time multimodal interaction. Going forward, I aim to develop expressive, full-duplex interactive multimodal models and inference-time reasoning techniques during live interaction, while advancing physical fidelity and long-horizon consistency to support real-world task planning.
\end{itemize}
