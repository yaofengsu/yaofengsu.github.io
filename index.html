About

I am an undergraduate at Fudan University and currently a Research Intern at Tencent, with research interests mainly in streaming interactive generation toward interactive world models. Previously, I was glad to work with the Echo Team at Joy Future Academy, JD, where our JoyAI-Echo achieved minute-long audio-video story generation, and my work OmniForcing was selected for a Long Oral at ECCV 2026 (0.27% of submissions) and presented in an invited talk to the Lightricks LTX-Video team, including their CTO and Director of GenAI Research. In 2025, I also spent a wonderful semester living in Chapel Hill at UNC, working with Prof. Huaxiu Yao on SimpleMem (ICML 2026).

I’m excited to begin my PhD with Prof. Yinghao Xu in Fall 2027. If you find my research interesting or would like to connect, feel free to reach out at yaofengsu@outlook.com.

Research Interests

My research interest lies in multimodal generative models as a means to build faithful representations of the world. Through reinforcement learning, distillation, and agent frameworks, I have contributed to applications spanning audio-visual generation, autonomous driving, and video understanding. I am particularly drawn to developing principled approaches that bridge generative modeling with physically grounded world understanding.
